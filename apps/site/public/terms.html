Terms, in plain words

These are the rules for using Buildpair while it's in early testing. Full terms will replace them before the public launch.

Who can use it

You need to be at least 16. One account per person, and keep your password to yourself.

Your stuff stays yours

What you write in Buildpair belongs to you. You let us store it and show it to the people you share it with, which is what the app is for. When you delete it, or delete your account, we stop doing that.

Be decent

  • No harassment, bullying, hate or threats.
  • No spam, scams or pretending to be someone else.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing sexual involving anyone under 18.
  • Don't try to break, overload or get around the app's limits and protections.

If someone breaks these rules, report them from their name in a space, or from the message itself. We look at reports within a day. We may remove content or close accounts that break the rules.

What we promise, and what we can't

We work hard to keep Buildpair running and your data safe. But it's early software, offered as it is, and things can go wrong. Keep your own copy of anything you can't lose. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for losses from using it.

Changes

We'll update these terms as Buildpair grows, and tell you in the app when something important changes. You can stop using Buildpair and delete your account at any time.
