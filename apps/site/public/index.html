How it works

  1. 1

    Make a space

    A space is a list and one chat: "Ledgerly" for the product you're shipping, "Life" for the family, "Running" for the half marathon. Add to-dos, habits and goals in plain words, like "gym 4 times a week".

  2. 2

    Invite your people

    Share a link with the friends, partners or fellow builders you want in your corner. Each space has its own people, and anything can stay visible only to you.

  3. 3

    Keep it moving

    Tick things off and the space's chat shows it. Skip a day and say why. When something goes quiet, your supporters get a nudge to check in, so it reads like a friend reaching out.

The chat is where it happens

Every space has one conversation. Finished items post themselves, small to-dos roll up into one line a day, and reactions stack so the chat stays readable. Updates on a goal land right next to the replies they get.

  • Messages arrive instantly and in order, even after a bad connection
  • Write offline; it sends when you're back
  • No feeds, no followers, no like counts

Private by default

Nothing is shared until you choose who's in a space. Any single item can stay visible only to you.

Small audiences

Accountability comes from a few people who know you, not from followers.

Room for bad weeks

Habits count weeks on target, not fragile streaks. A quiet goal gets noticed, never shamed.

No ads

The core app is free and sharing is always free. We don't sell your data or show ads.
