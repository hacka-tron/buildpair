Privacy, in plain words

This is how Buildpair handles your information today. A full privacy policy will replace it before the public launch.

What we keep

  • Your account. That's your name, email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), time zone, what you're building, and your sign-in sessions.
  • What you put in your spaces. That means to-dos, habits, goals, messages, reactions and weekly reflections. People in a space can see it. Items you mark "only visible to me" stay yours alone, and never reach anyone else's device.
  • Who's in which space, invite links, and requests to join.
  • People you've blocked, and reports you send. A report keeps a copy of the message you reported so it can be reviewed.
  • Your network address, for a minute or so, to slow down repeated sign-in attempts. It isn't stored with your account.

Your device also keeps a copy of your spaces and which messages you've read, so the app opens fast and works offline.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking pixels, and no selling or sharing your data for advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No usage analytics yet. If we add them, they'll be tied to an internal ID (never your name, your email, or what you write), and you'll be able to turn them off.

Your choices

  • Make any item private, or share it again.
  • Leave any space you joined, or remove people from yours.
  • Block someone so you don't see their messages and they can't ask to join your spaces.
  • Delete your account from your profile. Spaces you own are deleted for everyone in them. In other spaces, everything you made is removed, including from spaces you've left.

Where it lives

Buildpair runs on Cloudflare. Emails (sign-up codes, password resets and reports for review) are sent through Resend.
