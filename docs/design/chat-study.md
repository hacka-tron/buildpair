# What makes chat fun, and what Buildpair takes from it

Written 2026-10-16 after the owner asked for a chat that feels whimsical and fluid. It looks at WeChat, QQ, LINE, Telegram, iMessage, Snapchat, Discord and Slack. Chinese chat apps get the most attention, because they're known for being playful and cartoony.

## What the playful apps do

**WeChat "pat pat" (拍一拍).** Double-tap someone's avatar and the chat shows a small line, "Maya patted Jordan". Each person can set a playful ending of their own, such as "...on the head" or "...and told them to go for a run". It launched in June 2020 and was reportedly one line of code ([iFanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1358474)). Product writers call it a *weak reminder*, a way to say hello or cheer someone on that's lighter than a message ([woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/?p=4005877)). Its known flaw is that the ending is global, so the same joke goes to your boss and your best friend, and a double-tap is easy to do by accident.

**WeChat emoji rain (表情雨).** Some words make emoji fall across the screen. "生日快乐" (happy birthday) brings cakes, "财源滚滚" brings coins, and new ones arrive each Lunar New Year ([Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/34473.html), [iFanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/780735)). People send the words just to see it happen. It turns ordinary messages into small surprises.

**QQ sparks (火花) and friendship marks.** Message a friend three days in a row and a spark appears next to their name. It grows and darkens the longer it lasts, and goes out if a day is missed ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/2684660078083078)). In September 2026 QQ added a shared chat sprite that two friends raise together through talking ([Sina](https://news.sina.cn/sx/2026-09-23/detail-inisvcqf1200968.d.html)). Snapchat's streaks work the same way. They work because of shared commitment, and also because of the fear of losing them, which can feel like pressure.

**LINE stickers.** Big cartoon characters that show a feeling at a glance. LINE says the stickers that do best have clear emotion, everyday use and characters people recognize ([LINE](https://www.linecorp.com/pr/news/global/20260715/)). Researchers point out that a sticker can soften a message that would read as blunt in words ([Monocle](https://monocle.com/magazine/issues/110/cartoon-networks)).

**Telegram animated emoji.** A single emoji sent alone shows big and animated, and tapping it plays a full-screen effect for both people ([Emojipedia](https://blog.emojipedia.org/telegrams-animated-emoji-set/)). WhatsApp and iMessage also show emoji-only messages larger.

**iMessage effects.** Confetti, fireworks and balloons across the screen, and bubble effects like Slam and Invisible Ink. Apple turns them off when Reduce Motion is on ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/ht206894)).

**Discord and Slack.** Their delight is in the small things, like a typing indicator people joke about ("typing a novel") and friendly empty and loading states ([OpenClassrooms on Slack](https://openclassrooms.com/courses/4555931-create-simple-prototypes-with-wireframes/4908336-make-note-of-microinteractions)).

## The common thread

- **Small gestures cost less than words.** Pats, stickers and reactions let someone show they care without having to write something.
- **Surprise rewards attention.** Effects that only appear sometimes make people want to see what happens.
- **Shared progress feels like a relationship.** Sparks and sprites belong to the pair, not to one person.
- **Everything moves.** Bubbles pop in, typing dots bounce, and reactions burst. Stillness reads as broken.

These fit Buildpair unusually well. A supporter's whole job is to show up lightly and often, which is what a pat or a sticker does. And the moments that matter (finishing a goal, getting back on track) deserve a celebration.

## What Buildpair builds, in order

1. **A chat that feels alive.**
   - Your messages sit on the right in blue, other people's on the left.
   - A run of messages from one person groups under one avatar, with the time on the last one.
   - New messages pop in.
   - Typing shows bouncing dots with the person's name.
   - A message of just one to three emoji shows big, with a small bounce.
   - Double-tap a message to send a heart, with a burst.
   - Reactions pop when added.
2. **Celebrations.**
   - A finished to-do shows as a bright card with a 🙌 high-five button.
   - Finishing a goal throws confetti for everyone watching.
   - Words like "congrats", "happy birthday", "shipped", "launched" and "🎉" make emoji fall once, WeChat style.
   - All motion respects Reduce Motion.
3. **Nudges, like pat pat.**
   - Double-tap someone's avatar in the chat to nudge them, and the chat shows "Jordan nudged Maya 👋".
   - Each person can set a short ending per space, which avoids WeChat's global-ending problem.
   - Nudges are limited so they can't be spammed, and can be undone for a few seconds after a slip.
4. **Cheer stickers.** A built-in pack of big, colorful cards for supporters, such as "Proud of you", "Ship it", "Where you at?", "Rest day", "You got this" and "Let's go". Sent in one tap from beside the message box.
5. **The spark.**
   - A space lights a 🔥 with a day count when the owner made progress and a supporter answered, three or more days in a row. It grows warmer the longer it lasts.
   - Unlike Snapchat, a missed day dims it for a day before it goes out, so it encourages without punishing.

## Left out on purpose

- **Paid sticker stores and creator markets.** These are a business on their own and out of scope.
- **Avatars that copy your face.** That means camera, privacy and storage work for a small gain.
- **Global pat endings.** Endings are per space, so the joke fits the people.
